This blog is my notebook disguised as a website. It’s where my ideas go to stretch their legs, a place that I can put pen to paper to express myself, where logs are more than just lines of text, and where systems (both digital and human) are picked apart and put back together (sometimes in better shape, sometimes just louder).

I write here about the art and grit of detection engineering, threat hunting, and the occasional homelab misadventure. More recently, as AI has taken the world by storm I have been moving in that direction. "How can we better defend in a World where AI exists?" is my leading through process. If you like your tech with a side of curiosity and dry humor, you’ll probably feel at home.

From time to time, I may take detours into other rabbit holes; books, philosophy, strange patterns, or whatever else manages to hijack my attention. It keeps things interesting.

No growth hacks. No personal brands. Just experiments, reflections, and the occasional success worth documenting.

Thanks for stopping by. Poke around. Break things gently.
